The Star Light Experience

An intimate, low-light lounge with smooth hookah, good music, and a crowd that knows how to relax. Step inside a cosmic atmosphere where every wall tells a story.

Premium Selection

World-Class Hookah

We carry two of the most respected shisha brands in the world. Al Fakher brings 11 classic flavors, from the timeless Double Apple to refreshing Watermelon. Starbuzz delivers 9 premium options with bolder, more complex profiles like Blue Mist and Irish Kiss.

Every hookah is prepared fresh for your session. Our staff takes the time to pack each bowl properly, manage the heat, and make sure you get smooth, flavorful clouds from the first pull to the last.

The Space

Galaxy Walls and Moon Ceilings

Star Light Cafe is not just a place to smoke. The entire interior is hand-painted with galaxy murals, celebrity portraits, and cosmic designs that give the lounge a feel you will not find anywhere else in Northern Virginia.

Low lighting, leather seating, and a moon ceiling mural set the mood the moment you walk in. Whether you are here for a quiet evening or a louder weekend night, the atmosphere adjusts to the crowd without losing its character.

Pool Tables

Challenge your friends to a game of pool while your hookah is being prepared. The table stays open all night for customers.

Live Sports

Multiple screens streaming soccer matches, boxing, MMA, and major sporting events. This is a go-to spot for fight nights and soccer watch parties.

Music On Request

Your vibe, your playlist. Request any song and the team plays it. From Arabic classics to hip-hop to chill lo-fi, the music matches the mood you set.

Free Wi-Fi

Many of our regulars bring laptops and work from the lounge. Fast, free Wi-Fi for every customer. Productive and relaxed at the same time.

Bring Your Food

Outside food is always welcome. Grab dinner from a nearby restaurant and pair it with your hookah session. We have drinks covered with fresh juices, sodas, and Moroccan mint tea.

Open Late

Tuesday through Saturday, the lounge stays open until 1 AM. Sunday and Monday until midnight. Walk-ins always welcome, no reservation needed.

Come Through Tonight

Open daily from 5 PM (Sun and Mon from 6 PM). Walk-ins welcome. No reservation needed.

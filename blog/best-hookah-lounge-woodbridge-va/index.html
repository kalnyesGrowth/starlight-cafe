5 Reasons Star Light Cafe Is Woodbridge's Favorite Hookah Spot

Published August 5, 2026 · 7 min read

Woodbridge, Virginia has a handful of hookah options. There are cafes off Route 1, a few spots along Dale Boulevard, and the occasional restaurant that throws a hookah pipe on the menu as an afterthought. But ask anyone who goes out regularly, and one name comes up more than the rest.

Star Light Cafe has been a go-to for hookah smokers in the Woodbridge and Prince William County area for good reason. Here are five specific things that set it apart.

1. Premium Tobacco Only

This is the single biggest differentiator, and most customers don't realize it until they've smoked at a few different places.

Star Light Cafe carries two brands: Al Fakher and Starbuzz. Both are internationally recognized, premium-grade hookah tobacco. Al Fakher is the world's most popular hookah brand, produced in the United Arab Emirates with strict quality standards. Starbuzz is a California-based premium line known for creative, complex flavor blends.

Many lounges in the area supplement with cheaper, off-brand tobacco to increase margins. The difference is noticeable: off-brand shisha burns hotter, loses flavor faster, and often tastes harsh by the 20-minute mark. At Star Light, an Al Fakher session runs 45 to 60 minutes of clean, consistent flavor. A Starbuzz session can push past an hour. Not sure which to pick? Read our Al Fakher vs Starbuzz comparison for a full breakdown.

The menu includes 11 Al Fakher flavors (White Grape, Double Apple, Orange, Strawberry, Peach, Watermelon, Pineapple, Vanilla, Mint, Mango, Lemon) and 9 Starbuzz flavors (Blue Mist, White Mint, Gum, Sex on the Beach, Code69, Peach Mist, Irish Kiss, Melon Dew, Pan). Both brands are $26 + tax per device for one to two people, plus $5 for each additional person sharing. Check the full menu for the complete lineup.

2. The Atmosphere

Walk into Star Light Cafe and the first thing you notice is the space itself. The walls are hand-painted with galaxy murals. Deep blues, purples, and scattered stars wrap around the room. The ceiling features a large moon installation that anchors the whole cosmic theme.

The lighting is low and warm, the kind that makes everyone look good and makes your phone camera struggle in the best way. The seating is a mix of leather couches and standard tables, giving you options depending on whether you want to sink in or sit upright.

It doesn't feel like a strip mall hookah spot. It feels intentional. The owners clearly put money into the build-out, and it shows. For a lot of regulars, the atmosphere alone is the reason they come back. It's the kind of place you bring a date or meet up with friends when you want something more interesting than a bar but less committal than a full dinner.

3. Staff That Actually Cares

This sounds generic, but it's the most common theme across Google reviews. Rashidi, the owner, comes up by name again and again.

"Rashidi the owner of the place is the most friendliest person ever. Atmosphere was great and so many flavors to pick from. Calm and nice music." - Becks, Google Review

"I literally go here all of the time and for years! I recommend it 10/10! Muhammad always takes care of me. This is a chill vibe spot, I like to bring my laptop connect to the Wi-Fi, and chill out." - Leeana Hayes, Google Review

At a lot of lounges, you order, get your hookah, and then don't see the staff again unless you wave them down. At Star Light, the team rotates coals proactively, checks in on your session, and adjusts heat without being asked. They'll remember your order if you're a regular. They'll give you honest recommendations if you're new.

The music request thing is real. If you have a vibe in mind, they'll throw your playlist on the system. It's a small touch that makes a big difference, especially on a Friday night when you're there with your crew.

4. More Than Just Hookah

Hookah is the centerpiece, but Star Light Cafe gives you reasons to stay beyond the smoke session.

Pool tables. If you're there with friends and want something active between rounds, the pool table is always open. It breaks up the sitting and gives the group something to do besides scroll phones.

Live sports. Multiple TVs showing soccer, football, basketball, whatever is on. If you're catching a game and want to do it in a more interesting setting than your living room, it works well.

Free Wi-Fi. Plenty of people come in with laptops. The vibe is relaxed enough that you can work or study while smoking. During weekday evenings, it's common to see students and remote workers posted up.

Bring your own food. Outside food is welcome. That means you can grab takeout from wherever you want, bring it in, and pair it with your hookah. Many regulars order delivery straight to the lounge. The only restriction is outside drinks, since Star Light serves its own fresh juices, smoothies, and Moroccan mint tea.

5. Open Late, 7 Days a Week

A surprising number of hookah lounges in the area close early or only open on weekends. Star Light Cafe is open every single day.

  • Tuesday through Saturday: 5 PM to 1 AM
  • Sunday and Monday: 6 PM to 12 AM

The late hours matter. Most hookah sessions start after dinner, usually 8 PM or later. A lounge that closes at 11 PM gives you a two-hour window at best. Star Light stays open until 1 AM on weeknights and weekends, giving you the full session without watching the clock.

The 7-day availability also sets it apart. Sunday and Monday hookah options are limited in Woodbridge. Star Light being open on those days means you always have somewhere to go, not just on the "going out" nights.

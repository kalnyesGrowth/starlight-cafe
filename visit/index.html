Before You Come

House Rules

21 and Over Only

Federal tobacco law applies. Everyone in the lounge must be 21 or older and carry a valid government-issued ID. We check at the door every time, no exceptions.

Bring Your Own Food

Outside food is welcome. Order from any restaurant nearby, bring it in, and pair it with your session. Plenty of regulars do exactly that on weekends.

No Outside Drinks

Drinks have to come from us. We pour fresh juices, sodas, coffee, and Moroccan mint tea. Leave the cooler in the car and let us handle the refreshments.

Walk-Ins Welcome

No reservation system, no cover charge, no guest list. Show up when you want and we will find you a seat. Larger groups can call ahead so we can plan the setup.

See You Tonight

Open Tuesday to Saturday until 1 AM. Sunday and Monday until midnight. Walk-ins welcome, 21 and over.

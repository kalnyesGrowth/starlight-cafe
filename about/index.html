About Star Light Cafe

Woodbridge's go-to hookah lounge. A space built for people who want to relax, smoke premium shisha, and enjoy good company.

Our Story

Built for the Community

Star Light Cafe started with a simple idea: give Woodbridge a place where people can come, sit down, smoke great hookah, and be themselves. No pretense. No dress code. Just a comfortable lounge with premium shisha, good music, and a crowd that knows how to unwind.

Rashidi built the space by hand. The galaxy-painted walls, the moon ceiling mural, the leather seating arranged for conversation. Every detail was chosen to create an atmosphere you can feel the moment you walk in. The lighting stays low. The hookah stays smooth. The music plays whatever you want to hear.

What started as a neighborhood spot has become a destination. Regulars come back week after week. New visitors become regulars after one session. Word spreads because the experience speaks for itself.

What We Offer

Everything You Need for a Great Night

From premium tobacco to a full drink menu, Star Light Cafe has everything covered. Here is what you will find when you visit.

Premium Shisha

20 flavors across two world-class brands. Al Fakher is our classic line with 11 flavors. Starbuzz is the premium option with 9 bold, unique blends. Every bowl prepared fresh.

Full Drink Menu

Hot teas including our signature Moroccan mint, fresh juices, sodas, energy drinks, and Middle Eastern specialties. Over 25 drinks to choose from.

Pool Tables

Challenge your friends to a game while your hookah is being prepared. Our pool tables stay busy all night long.

Live Sports

Multiple screens streaming live soccer and sports events. Catch every match while you smoke. The weekend crowd gets loud in the best way.

Free Wi-Fi

Bring your laptop and work remote. Many regulars treat Star Light as their evening office. Strong, free Wi-Fi for all customers.

BYOF Friendly

Bring your own food. Order from the restaurant next door, bring dinner from home, whatever works. Outside food is always welcome. Outside drinks are not.

Community

More Than a Hookah Lounge

Star Light Cafe is a meeting point for Woodbridge. Business partners hold meetings over hookah. Friends catch up on weeknights. Solo visitors settle in with a laptop and a teapot for the evening. The atmosphere draws people in and the community keeps them coming back.

Muhammad takes care of every customer personally. He remembers your name, your preferred flavor, and how you like your tea poured. That level of service is what turns a first visit into a regular habit.

With 4.2 stars on Google and 119 reviews, the reputation speaks for itself. But the best way to understand Star Light is to come through. Walk in any evening and see why Woodbridge chose this as their spot.
